Need Help?

Find support during the Spring 2026 Job Fair

If you need support during the event, PA CareerLink® staff are here to help. All PA CareerLink® services are provided at no cost to job seekers.

Find the PA CareerLink® Help Tables

Visit the PA CareerLink® help tables during the event for one-on-one support. If you are unsure where to go, event staff can direct you to the nearest PA CareerLink® table.

Support Available

  • Resume help
  • Interview tips
  • Job search guidance
  • Planning your next step
  • General PA CareerLink® questions

Already Requested Help?

If you requested assistance during registration, please go directly to the PA CareerLink® help tables when you arrive so staff can connect with you quickly.

Career Services & Training Opportunities

After the job fair, PA CareerLink® Blair County can help you continue your progress with no cost services for job seekers, including:

  • Training funding for in-demand careers
  • On-the-Job Training
  • Paid work experience for youth and young adults
  • Career planning and skill assessments
  • Workshops and certifications
  • Support services to help people stay on track

Visit the help tables today or connect with PA CareerLink® after the event to get started.

Help is available at no cost, and no appointment is needed during the event.
